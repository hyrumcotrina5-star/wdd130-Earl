About Me

I am religious, and growing up, my parents shielded my sisters and me from certain things in media. I am grateful to them for that, and I plan on doing the same with my future children.

I know I can't protect my kids from everything, so I plan on evolving what I present to them as they grow up. There is so much media in today’s world, and I knew I wanted to make something like this website for myself. But I figured, why not let others use it too?

This website exists to help people quickly find books, movies, and songs that match their values. Instead of relying on a single rating system, you can turn specific content filters on or off, such as violence, drug use, profanity, and sexual content. The goal is to save people time, especially parents, and help them make informed choices without having to research everything themselves.

Hopefully, this makes it easy for you to discover entertainment with confidence and help parents choose content that is appropriate for their children.
